I am a Lecturer in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the School of Electrical, Electronic and Mechanical Engineering, University of Bristol, UK, where I started this role in January 2024.  Before joining Bristol, I was a FRQNT Research Postdoc Fellow at McGill University, Montreal, QC, Canada and Mitacs Industrial Postdoc Fellow at École Polytechnique de Montréal, Montreal, QC, Canada. I received the Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from the École Polytechnique de Montréal, Montreal, QC, Canada, in 2020, the M.Eng. degree in Optical Engineering from Zhejiang University, Hangzhou, China, in 2015, and the B.Eng. degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), Chengdu, China, in 2012. 

My research focuses on emerging energy-efficient wireless technologies, including wireless power transfer, backscattering, joint sensing and communications, IoT, MMIC, and nonlinear circuit analysis and design.
